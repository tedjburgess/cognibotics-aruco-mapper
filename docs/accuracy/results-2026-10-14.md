## Setup and reference uncertainty

The tester used an Android Samsung S25 and printed markers. The implementation maps black-square sides to 20 mm (4×4), 50 mm (5×5), 75 mm (6×6) and 100 mm (7×7). These three runs show IDs 0 and 1 from the 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7 families; they do not establish physical performance for the small 20 mm 4×4 family.

**The manual references have an estimated uncertainty of ±3 mm**, covering ruler placement, locating the marker centres and possible sheet movement. This is the tester's estimate, not a verified bound, instrument specification, standard deviation or 95% confidence interval. Repeated reference measurements were not supplied. No error has been reduced by subtracting 3 mm.

The means of a few millimetres are comparable to that reference uncertainty estimate. This experiment therefore cannot cleanly separate app error from manual reference error at that scale. Do not call the combined result “98.9% accuracy.”

The camera model is estimated from camera metadata, not a measured checkerboard calibration; the screen labels that limitation. Evidence concerns the #57 implementation based on `84b5e27`, before the commits containing this report. Camera distance, analysis resolution and independently measured camera angles were not supplied.

## Calculation

For each pair:

- Signed error: `app − reference`.
- Absolute error: `|app − reference|`.
- Absolute percentage error: `100 × |app − reference| / reference`.

For the eight cross-height pairs in runs 1 and 2, the reference is `sqrt(horizontal_spacing² + 43²)` mm, using the reported 43 mm centre-height difference. The four 6×6-to-7×7 references and three same-sheet references are transcribed directly. This assumes the written horizontal spacings are horizontal centre-to-centre projections and the stated height difference applies to those eight pairs.

Calculations use the original numbers and unrounded square roots, then round only for display. The handwritten rounded square-root answers and percentages are not reused; a few do not match the written inputs.

## Limitations and other observations

- The first two runs contain displayed, apparently smoothed results, while the selected run 3 segment shows raw results. The combined figures describe the supplied readings across these conditions; they are not a controlled repeatability study using identical settings.
- No new fourth run is implied by the supplementary steeper-angle observations later in run 3. At about 100 seconds its nominal 135 mm 7×7 #0 → #1 pair displays about **140 mm**, roughly **3.7%** difference. Steady display is enabled there. This is not an angle-specific mean or a maximum over every frame.
- Marker pairs share markers, errors can be correlated, and the physical arrangement changes between runs.
- Tilt shown by the app comes from the same solver; it is not an independent camera-angle measurement.
- The overlay becomes displaced after a phone orientation change in the video. Green outlines and tracking percentages do not establish geometric accuracy.
- Some readings are missing/rejected during the video. A full valid/failed-attempt count or detection rate has not been calculated; the tables must not be presented as evidence of 100% detection.
- Original screenshots, notebook photos and video are held by the tester and are not uploaded to the repository. The source names above identify the supplied evidence.

# Initial physical measurement results — #39 / #57

Test evidence supplied: 14 October 2026. Report written: 15 October 2026.

## Setup and reference uncertainty

The tester used an Android Samsung S25 phone and printed markers. The implementation uses black-square side lengths of 20 mm (4×4), 50 mm (5×5), 75 mm (6×6) and 100 mm (7×7). The latest recording shows six markers: IDs 0 and 1 from each of the 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7 families. It does not establish performance for the smaller 20 mm 4×4 markers.

The tester kept the sheets as fixed as possible and supplied 12 manually measured centre-to-centre distances in mm, in the app's order, excluding the three same-sheet pairs. Each same-sheet pair has a nominal printed spacing of 135 mm. No additional elevation correction was applied to the latest 12 supplied distances; they are treated as the reference distances for that arrangement.

**Reference distances have an estimated uncertainty of ±3 mm**, allowing for ruler placement, locating the marker centres and possible paper movement during measurement. This is the tester's estimate, not a verified bound, instrument specification, standard deviation or 95% confidence interval. Repeated reference measurements were not supplied. Keep all calculated errors unchanged; do not subtract 3 mm or dismiss smaller differences as proof of zero app error.

The camera model is estimated from camera metadata, not a measured checkerboard calibration. The live view labels this limitation. This test concerns the uncommitted #57 implementation based on `84b5e27`; it predates the commit containing this report. Camera distance, analysis resolution and independent physical angle measurements were not recorded, so results must not be presented as a controlled calibration or working-distance guarantee.

- Mean absolute error: **2.38 mm**.
- Mean absolute percentage error: **0.89%** (arithmetic mean of the 12 individual percentages).

In particular, the mean absolute difference is smaller than that reference uncertainty estimate, so this experiment cannot cleanly separate app error from reference error at that scale.

## Other observations

- The earlier photos include a raised-sheet setup with a reported 43 mm marker-centre height difference. The tester compared those readings with `sqrt(horizontal_spacing² + 43²)`. These are preliminary observations of different-height measurements, kept separate from the latest table; the horizontal references and repeatability still need controlled verification.
- Later in the recording the camera is much more oblique. At about 100 seconds the 7×7 #0 → #1 displayed distance is about **140.0 mm**, versus the nominal **135 mm** spacing (about **3.7%** difference). Steady display is enabled in that section. This single observation is not an angle-specific mean or maximum over all frames.
- Displayed tilt is an estimate from the same pose solver, not an independent measurement of the test angle. It cannot establish that the requested 0°, 15°, 35° and 60° trials have been completed.
- The overlay becomes displaced after a phone orientation change in the recording. Preview-coordinate alignment remains a known UI limitation; visible green outlines and tracking percentages do not prove geometric accuracy.
- Missing/rejected readings appear in parts of the recording. No complete count of valid/failed attempts or detection-rate estimate has been made. Do not omit failed attempts from the planned controlled trials.
